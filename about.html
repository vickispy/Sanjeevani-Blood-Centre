About Sanjeevani Blood Centre

Founded in 2019 in Bhosari, Pune, driven by our guiding ethos — "The Greatest Noble Aim" — to bring reliable, safe, and compassionate blood transfusion support to every patient in need.

Our Origins & Philosophy

Rooted in Pune, Dedicated to Saving Lives

Sanjeevani Blood Centre was established in 2019 in Bhosari, Pune, Maharashtra. Recognizing that access to safe blood and blood components is a fundamental pillar of emergency medicine, oncological care, maternal health, and chronic illness management, our founders set out to build an institution defined by clinical excellence, complete transparency, and donor comfort.

The name Sanjeevani is inspired by the timeless symbol of restorative life. Our motto, "The Greatest Noble Aim," reminds our staff and volunteer network that voluntary blood donation represents humanity at its most selfless: an anonymous gift from one human being to preserve the life of another.

Our Guiding Pillars

Mission, Vision & Organisational Values

Every operational protocol, donor interaction, and component distribution reflects our uncompromising commitment to healthcare ethics.

Our Mission

To mobilize, inspire, and care for voluntary blood donors; to separate whole blood into high-purity therapeutic components with uncompromising quality controls; and to ensure safe, rapid, and equitable availability of blood products to hospitals, clinicians, and patients across Maharashtra.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a premier center of excellence in transfusion medicine, voluntary donor engagement, and humanitarian support in Western India — where no patient suffers due to the non-availability of compatible, screened blood.

Core Institutional Values

  • Compassion: Caring with respect for every donor and patient.
  • Safety First: 100% sterile, single-use phlebotomy kits.
  • Integrity: Honest reporting, regulatory compliance, zero commercialization.
  • Community: Collaborating with corporations, colleges, and civic groups.
