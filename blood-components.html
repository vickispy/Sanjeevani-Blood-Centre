Blood Components & Separation

Whole blood is a complex biological tissue. By separating it into individual therapeutic components, each donation targets specific clinical needs with maximized efficacy.

Clinical Availability Notice: The descriptions below are for educational and healthcare stakeholder reference. Specific component availability is subject to live inventory, authorized clinical requisitions, and statutory medical prescription.

Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP)

approx. 55% of blood volume

Rich in clotting factors, albumin, and immunoglobulins. Crucial for massive trauma, severe burn resuscitation, liver disease, and reversal of coagulopathy.

Detailed Catalogue

Therapeutic Blood Components

All components are processed under certified Good Manufacturing Practices and maintained within strict cold-chain ranges.

Cellular Component

Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBC)

Concentrated red blood cells obtained by centrifuging whole blood and removing the majority of plasma. Restores oxygen-carrying capacity in patients with severe acute blood loss or chronic severe anaemia.

Storage: 2°C to 6°C
Shelf Life: Up to 42 days (in CPDA-1/Additive solution)
Key Indications: Thalassemia, Sickle Cell, major surgery, severe trauma.
Inquire Availability
Liquid Component

Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP)

Liquid portion of whole blood frozen within 6 to 8 hours of collection to preserve labile clotting factors (Factor V and Factor VIII), albumin, and fibrinogen.

Storage: ≤ -30°C in dedicated plasma freezers
Shelf Life: Up to 1 year
Key Indications: Multiple coagulation factor deficiencies, severe burns, massive transfusions.
Inquire Availability
Platelet Component

Random Donor Platelets (RDP)

Platelets prepared from a single unit of whole blood through differential light and heavy centrifugation. Used in pooled doses to treat thrombocytopenia and control bleeding.

Storage: 20°C to 24°C under continuous gentle agitation
Shelf Life: 5 days
Key Indications: Dengue with severe thrombocytopenia, surgical bleeding, aplastic anaemia.
Inquire Availability
Apheresis Component

Single Donor Platelets (SDP)

Collected from a single voluntary donor using an automated cell separator (apheresis). Yields a therapeutic dose equivalent to 6–8 RDP units from a single donor, minimizing recipient antigen exposure.

Storage: 20°C to 24°C under continuous gentle agitation
Shelf Life: 5 days
Key Indications: Oncology, leukemia chemotherapy, refractory thrombocytopenia.
Inquire Availability
Factor Concentrate

Cryoprecipitate (CRYO)

The cold-insoluble protein fraction of FFP recovered by controlled thawing at 1°C–6°C and subsequent centrifugation. Highly enriched in Factor VIII, von Willebrand factor, and fibrinogen.

Storage: ≤ -30°C
Shelf Life: Up to 1 year
Key Indications: Hypofibrinogenemia, DIC, Factor XIII deficiency, uremic bleeding.
Inquire Availability
SWPRBC
Saline Washed PRBC
Terminology Confirmation Pending

Saline Washed PRBC (SWPRBC)

Red cells washed with sterile normal saline to remove plasma proteins, antibodies, and residual leukocytes. Recommended for patients with severe recurring allergic reactions or IgA deficiency.

Note: The exact terminology (SWPRBC) and facility preparation protocol are subject to formal confirmation with the centre's medical director.
