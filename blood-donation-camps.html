Corporate & Community Blood Donation Camps

Partner with Sanjeevani Blood Centre to host an organized, hygienic, and impactful blood donation camp at your corporate campus, IT park, college, or housing society.

CSR & Community Leadership

The Value of Corporate Blood Drives

A turnkey workplace donation drive fosters team cohesion, corporate social responsibility, and saves vital lives in Pune hospitals.

Zero Operational Disruption

We manage the entire on-site clinical workflow. Employees donate in comfortable 15-minute time slots without disrupting regular business operations.

Turnkey Medical Logistics

Our mobile team arrives fully equipped with specialized donor couches, sterile single-use kits, emergency medical kits, doctor screening counters, and refreshments.

Recognition & Appreciation

Every voluntary donor receives a formal Certificate of Appreciation and Donor Card. A memento and Certificate of Partnership is presented to the host organization.

Direct Thalassemia Support

Every unit collected powers our reported humanitarian commitment to provide more than 200 free units monthly to Thalassemia and Sickle Cell patients.

Coordination Sequence

5 Steps to a Successful Camp

How our mobile phlebotomy team plans and executes on-site blood donation camps.

1

Initial Enquiry

Reach out with proposed dates, venue location, and expected number of participants.

2

Feasibility Review

Our coordinator reviews the space, date alignment, and medical team availability.

3

Awareness Drive

We supply digital banners, FAQ mailers, and pre-donation guidelines for your staff.

4

Logistics Setup

Our team arrives 1 hour prior to setup clean donor couches, screening, and recovery areas.

5

Execution & Care

Hygienic collection, donor refreshments, certificate distribution, and safe cold transit.

Basic Venue Requirements for Host Organisations

  • • Clean, well-ventilated room (approx. 400 to 600 sq. ft. or more, depending on expected attendance).
  • • Air-conditioning or good fan ventilation to keep donors cool and comfortable.
  • • Tables & chairs: 2–3 tables for registration and doctor screening, and chairs for waiting.
  • • Drinking water supply accessible for donors.
