The Gift of Life: Donor Guide

Every donation has the potential to save up to three lives. Explore preparation steps, the 5-step donation journey, and aftercare guidance.

Medical Notice: The eligibility guidance provided below is for general awareness. Official blood donor eligibility and deferral decisions are made exclusively on-site by authorized healthcare personnel in accordance with current statutory guidelines.
Before You Arrive

How to Prepare for Your Donation Visit

A few simple, healthy steps before your appointment ensure a comfortable and smooth experience.

Eat an Appropriate Meal

Have a healthy meal or light nutritious snack 2 to 3 hours before donation. Never donate on an empty stomach or after prolonged fasting.

Drink Sufficient Water

Hydration is crucial. Drink an extra 500ml of water or fruit juice before donating to maintain healthy circulatory volume.

Get Adequate Rest

Ensure at least 6 to 8 hours of sound sleep the night prior to donation to feel refreshed and energized.

Bring Official Photo ID

Carry a valid government-issued identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN card, Driving Licence, or Passport) for mandatory registration.

Answer Questions Honestly

Truthfully answer all confidential medical questions regarding medications, recent travels, or past ailments to protect both yourself and the recipient.

Follow Medical Team Instructions

Our trained phlebotomists and medical officers will guide you through each stage for your total comfort.

The Complete Journey

What Happens During Your Visit

A transparent, relaxed, 35-to-45 minute clinical protocol designed around donor comfort.

1

Step 1: Reception & Registration

Our reception team welcomes you, checks your identity credentials, and provides a confidential Donor Questionnaire to record demographic details and general wellness history.

Time: 5–7 Minutes
2

Step 2: Confidential Medical Examination

A private vital signs screening evaluates your weight, pulse, blood pressure, temperature, and a rapid micro-capillary hemoglobin check (minimum required: 12.5 g/dL).

Time: 5–10 Minutes
3

Step 3: Blood Donation Procedure

You recline comfortably in an adjustable phlebotomy donor couch. Using a brand-new, sterile, sealed single-use blood bag with anticoagulant (CPDA-1), approximately 350ml or 450ml of whole blood is gently drawn. The collection itself takes only 8 to 10 minutes.

Collection Time: 8–10 Minutes
4

Step 4: Observation, Rest & Refreshments

You are guided to our recovery lounge to rest for 10–15 minutes. Enjoy fruit juice and light snacks while our medical staff observes you to ensure you feel 100% steady and comfortable.

Rest Time: 10–15 Minutes
5

Step 5: Processing, Screening & Patient Delivery

Your donated blood undergoes certified screening for mandatory infectious markers and is centrifuged into PRBC, fresh frozen plasma, and platelets to help multiple patients.

Laboratory Processing

After-Donation Care & Guidance

• Keep the adhesive bandage dry and in place for at least 4 to 6 hours.

• Hydrate generously: Drink an extra 3 to 4 glasses of water or fluids over the remainder of the day.

• Avoid heavy lifting, intense gym workouts, or vigorous physical exertion for the next 24 hours.

• Avoid smoking for 2 hours and refrain from alcohol consumption for the day.

• If you feel light-headed or dizzy: Lie down immediately with your legs slightly elevated, or sit down with your head between your knees until the sensation passes.
