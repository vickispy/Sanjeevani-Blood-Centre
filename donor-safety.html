Uncompromising Donor Safety

At Sanjeevani Blood Centre, donor safety is our primary clinical obligation. We follow stringent statutory protocols before, during, and after every blood donation.

Adherence to National Blood Transfusion Council Guidelines

All procedures, sanitization practices, and equipment selections strictly comply with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act (1940), FDA Maharashtra regulations, and established Good Laboratory Practices.

Phase 1

Before Donation

Safety begins before a needle ever touches skin. We conduct comprehensive screening to ensure that donation is medically appropriate for you.

  • Confidential Health History: Detailed questionnaire assessing medical background, medications, and recent infections.
  • Vital Signs Verification: Temperature, blood pressure, and pulse checked by trained medical staff.
  • Hemoglobin Testing: Rapid micro-lancet screening to ensure hemoglobin is ≥ 12.5 g/dL before collection.
Phase 2

During Donation

The collection procedure is performed in an aseptic, temperature-controlled environment with certified phlebotomists.

  • 100% Sterile Single-Use Kits: Every blood bag, needle, and alcohol swab is factory-sealed, opened in your presence, and immediately destroyed after use.
  • Certified Phlebotomists: Experienced healthcare technicians dedicated to minimizing discomfort.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Staff is by your side throughout the 8–10 minute collection to monitor comfort.
Phase 3

After Donation

Post-donation recovery is structured to ensure donors re-hydrate, regain physical equilibrium, and receive clear guidance.

  • Supervised Rest: Mandatory 10–15 minute relaxation in our recovery lounge.
  • Fluid Replenishment: Nutritious juice, water, and snacks provided to assist circulatory recovery.
  • Support If Unwell: Clear guidance on what to do if light-headedness occurs, with our 24/7 medical helpline.
Responsible Transparency

Clinical Facts & Common Concerns

We believe in transparent, evidence-based healthcare communication without exaggerated guarantees.

Can a donor contract an infection from donating blood?

No. It is impossible to contract HIV, Hepatitis, or any blood-borne virus by donating blood. Every single instrument used during donation — from the needle to the collection pack — is factory sterilized, vacuum-sealed, used for one individual donor, and immediately disposed of in statutory bio-medical puncture-proof sharps containers in accordance with the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules.

Does blood donation make you permanently weak?

No. The human body naturally replenishes the donated volume rapidly. The fluid volume (plasma) is typically restored within 24 to 48 hours with adequate hydration. Red blood cells are replaced by the bone marrow over a few weeks. The amount of blood taken (approx. 350ml to 450ml) represents less than 8% to 10% of an adult's total circulating volume and is safely tolerated by healthy adults.
