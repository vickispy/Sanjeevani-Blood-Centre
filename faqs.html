Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, clinical, and transparent answers regarding voluntary blood donation, donor eligibility criteria, safety standards, and camp hosting.

General Information Only: The answers below provide general public education. Final medical eligibility is determined exclusively on-site by authorized healthcare personnel in accordance with current statutory guidelines.

1. Donor Eligibility & Health Criteria

Generally, healthy individuals aged between 18 and 65 years, weighing at least 45 kg (or 50 kg for certain whole-blood bag volumes and apheresis), with a hemoglobin level of 12.5 g/dL or higher, are eligible for clinical evaluation. Normal pulse, blood pressure, and body temperature must also be confirmed during on-site screening.
Under standard Indian regulatory guidelines, there is a mandatory deferral period of 6 to 12 months following a tattoo, cosmetic piercing, or acupuncture procedure. This window period ensures that any potential blood-borne infection can be safely ruled out.
Women may donate during routine menstruation provided they feel completely well and meet the minimum hemoglobin threshold of 12.5 g/dL. Blood donation is deferred throughout pregnancy and for at least 12 months postpartum / during breastfeeding to safeguard mother and infant nutrition.
Individuals with well-controlled hypertension or type-2 diabetes managed through oral medication (without insulin) and with stable vital signs may typically donate, subject to on-site medical evaluation by our medical officer.

2. Donation Day Safety & Procedure

Absolutely none. Every needle, syringe, collection bag, and test lancet is 100% sterile, factory sealed, used exactly once for you, and immediately incinerated or disposed of in statutory biohazard sharps containers. It is physically impossible to contract any virus by donating blood.
A standard collection draws either 350ml or 450ml of whole blood, depending on your body weight. This represents less than 10% of an adult's blood volume. Your plasma volume is replenished within 24 to 48 hours with adequate fluids, and red cells are restored naturally by bone marrow within 4 to 6 weeks.
Male donors can donate whole blood every 90 days (3 months), and female donors can donate every 120 days (4 months). For platelet apheresis donors, the interval is shorter (typically 14 days), subject to clinical platelet count verification.

3. Blood Donation Camps & CSR

You only need to provide a well-lit, air-conditioned or well-ventilated room (approx. 400+ sq. ft.), 2–3 tables, chairs, and a clean drinking water facility. Sanjeevani Blood Centre brings all medical equipment, beds, sterile kits, medical officers, refreshments, and certificates.
We recommend contacting our coordination desk at least 7 to 14 days prior to your preferred date so that staff scheduling, digital awareness mailers, and logistics can be seamlessly aligned.
