PATIENT SUPPORT INITIATIVE

Free Blood Support for Thalassemia & Sickle Cell Patients

Patients living with Thalassemia Major and Sickle Cell Anaemia depend on frequent, life-sustaining blood transfusions. At Sanjeevani Blood Centre, we are dedicated to providing more than 200 units of blood free of charge every month to assist these courageous patients and their families.

Every single voluntary blood donation at our centre or community camps directly supports this noble humanitarian mission.

Read Impact Report Become a Regular Donor

Why Ongoing Support Is Crucial

  • Thalassemia patients often require blood transfusions every 2 to 4 weeks throughout their lives.
  • Fresh, washed, leukodepleted PRBC units help minimize transfusion-related reactions.
  • Providing units free of cost lifts an immense recurring financial burden from vulnerable families.
Monthly reported unit count subject to administrative hospital cross-verification.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential facts about voluntary blood donation eligibility and safety.

Generally, healthy individuals between 18 and 65 years of age, weighing at least 45 kg (or 50 kg for certain component donations), and with a hemoglobin level of 12.5 g/dL or above are evaluated for donation. Final eligibility is determined during confidential on-site medical screening by our clinical team.
Donating blood involves a brief pinch at the venepuncture site. All needles and collection bags are brand-new, sterile, and single-use, opened in front of you and disposed of into biomedical waste containers immediately. There is zero risk of contracting infections from donating blood.
For whole blood donation, male donors may typically donate every 3 months (90 days), and female donors every 4 months (120 days). Platelet apheresis donors may donate more frequently, subject to authorized platelet counts and clinical protocols.
The entire visit takes approximately 30 to 45 minutes, which includes registration, medical checkup, 8–10 minutes of actual collection, and 15 minutes of rest and refreshments.
