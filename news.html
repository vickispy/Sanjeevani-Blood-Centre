Demystifying Single Donor Platelet (SDP) Apheresis

Platelets are tiny cell fragments essential for blood clotting. In leukemia chemotherapy and complex surgeries, a single concentrated SDP donation can make all the difference. Discover how the apheresis process works and donor criteria.
