Component Processing

Advanced Blood Component Preparation

Modern transfusion medicine dictates that patients receive only the specific component their condition requires. We separate whole blood into Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBC), Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP), Random Donor Platelets (RDP), Single Donor Platelets (SDP), and Cryoprecipitate.

1 donated unit can thus provide therapeutic relief to up to three distinct recipients.

Clinical Diagnostics

Immunohematology & Infectious Marker Screening

Our testing laboratory performs comprehensive testing on every collected unit to confirm ABO & Rh typing, antibody screening, and crossmatching before release to clinicians.

Reported Laboratory Methods:

  • • Chemiluminescence (ECL): High-sensitivity automated immunoassay screening for infectious disease markers.
  • • ID-NAT (Individual Donor Nucleic Acid Testing): Advanced molecular screening method designed to shorten window periods for viral detection.
  • • Gel Card Crossmatch: Column agglutination technology for reliable, standardized pre-transfusion compatibility testing.
The precise operational scope, current on-site availability, and authorized usage of ECL and ID-NAT are subject to ongoing administrative confirmation.
